# Codex review brief — LUMERA

Review the entire repository as a principal editorial art director, senior frontend engineer, ecommerce UX reviewer and accessibility QA. This is a full-product review, not a diff review.

## Intent

LUMERA should feel like a Laboratory of Morning Light: premium ingestible beauty, Mediterranean morning, warm ivory, mineral green, copper rose and peach light. Its narrative is Light → Formula → Ritual → Editions. It must not resemble generic botanical wellness or a standard DTC card grid.

## First pass: audit only

Do not modify files on the first pass. Serve the repository locally and inspect the complete page at 390×844, 768×1024 and 1440×900. Test every edition CTA, bag quantity control, remove action, Escape behavior and demo checkout. Check browser console, failed requests, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion and horizontal overflow.

Pay special attention to:

1. Whether the approved product photograph is used elegantly without repetitive or awkward crops.
2. Whether the page feels editorial, tactile and premium rather than templated.
3. Whether the first mobile viewport communicates brand, real product and action immediately.
4. Palette discipline: lime may only be a small action/dosage accent.
5. Typography, spacing, contrast, hierarchy and rhythm between calm and dense sections.
6. Formula accuracy, responsible claims, supplement caveats and fictional-demo disclosure.
7. Semantic HTML, focus management, alt text and bag accessibility.

## Required report

Return findings ordered as Blocker, High, Medium and Polish. Every finding must include evidence: file and line, viewport, exact reproduction steps and expected behavior. Separate objective defects from subjective art-direction recommendations. End with a minimal repair plan in dependency order. Do not praise the page or invent problems to fill categories.

After Gustavo approves the report, implement only the approved fixes, rerun the same matrix and include before/after evidence.
