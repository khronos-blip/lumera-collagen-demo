Chapter III

The daily sequence

A bright minute.

Make the method small. Keep the pleasure tactile. Let consistency do the quiet work.

Measure

Add one level serving according to final pack directions. Precision first; excess is not progress.

Pour

Use 250 ml of cool water in a clear glass and leave a little room for movement.

Stir

Mix until smooth. Citrus Glow is imagined as clean and bright, never confectionary.

Return

Place the ritual beside a habit already in motion: breakfast, a notebook, or morning light.
